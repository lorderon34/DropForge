From campaigns to rewards

Game priorities and profiles

Search the catalog by name, group games into priority and exclusion lists, and save profiles to switch between them.

Campaign deadlines at a glance

Compare your remaining progress time with each reward's availability window. Export agenda dates in iCalendar format.

Progress in a floating overlay

The Mini HUD shows reward progress and estimated time remaining. Switch to compact mode to save screen space.

Reward history

View confirmed rewards in the local achievement journal, filter by date, and export your history as CSV.

Get started

  1. Download the Windows ZIP and extract it to a folder where you can write files.
  2. Open DropForge.exe and use Settings → Twitch Account to sign in through your browser.
  3. Browse campaigns and prioritize the games you want to follow.
